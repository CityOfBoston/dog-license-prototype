Version

V1

Stable

Initial end-to-end prototype covering the full licensing application flow — new license and renewal paths, license lookup by number or owner name, dog information with accordion cards, review, and payment handoff.

  • Lookup by license number returns a single matched dog.
  • Lookup by owner name returns matched dog + other dogs on the account.
  • Matched and related dogs are individually opt-in for renewal.
  • Find your license number skips the new/renewal path selector.
  • Up to 3 dogs per application; new and renewals can be combined.
URL https://cityofboston.github.io/dog-license-prototype/v1/ Open V1

Version

V2

Archived

Builds on V1. Explored the owner age-waiver path: a 70+ checkbox on the owner information step that surfaces the reduced-fee or waiver option for qualifying seniors.

  • Adds a "I am 70 or older" checkbox to the owner information step.
  • When checked, reflects eligibility for the senior waiver in the fee summary.
