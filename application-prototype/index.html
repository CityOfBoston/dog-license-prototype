    Boston requires all dog owners to license their dogs each year. A license helps Animal Control support rabies prevention, protect public health, and identify your pet.

    Before you start

    • Your dog must have proof of rabies vaccination.
    • Puppies must be licensed when they reach six months of age.
    • You must live in Boston to license your dog through the City of Boston.
    • You may be subject to a fine if your dog is not licensed.

    What you'll need

    Rabies vaccination certificate A photo or PDF of your dog's current rabies vaccination certificate from your veterinarian.
    Credit or debit card Visa, Mastercard, American Express, or Discover. A 2.75% service charge applies, with a $1 minimum.

    License period

    Dog licenses are valid from April 1 through March 31 each year.

    License fees

    Dog typeSexAnnual license fee
    Spayed or neutered dogFemale or male$15
    Unspayed or unneutered dogFemale or male$30

    Online payments have a 2.75% service charge, with a $1 minimum. Accepted cards: American Express, Discover, Mastercard, and Visa.

    Or apply in person or by mail

    Questions? Email Animal Care and Control at animalcontrol@boston.gov.

    Before you continue

    To complete your application:

    • You must be at least 18 years old.
    • You must provide proof of your dog's current rabies vaccination.
    • You must provide proof that your dog has been spayed or neutered, if applicable.
    • You must pay any outstanding violations before your application can be completed.
    • If the required certificates are not already on file, you must provide them during the application process. Missing documentation may delay or prevent approval.

    Invoice Cloud

    In the live application, users are handed off to Invoice Cloud here to complete payment by credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, or Discover).

    This prototype does not connect to Invoice Cloud and does not collect or process payment information.

    Open question — post-payment behavior not yet designed

    What happens after Invoice Cloud processes payment? The return URL, confirmation screen, license issuance timing, failed-payment handling, and partial-payment behavior are not confirmed. Do not assume completing payment means the license has been issued.
