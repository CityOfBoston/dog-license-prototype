License Your Dog

If you live in Boston and your dog is six months or older, you must get a dog license each year. Boston Animal Care and Control issues dog licenses.

Apply online, by mail, or in person. You'll need your dog's current rabies certificate, and any outstanding Animal Care and Control fines must be cleared before applying by mail or in person.

Who can apply

Boston residents can apply for a new dog license or renew an existing one. All dogs six months of age or older must be licensed annually.

Before you apply

Before you start, have these items ready:

  • ✓ Current rabies certificate — required for all applications
  • ✓ Proof of spay or neuter — required if your dog is altered, to qualify for the $15 fee
  • ✓ Payment — the license fee, plus any outstanding Animal Care and Control fines (fines must be cleared before applying by mail or in person)

You must license your dog each year before April 1. Failure to license may result in a $50 fine.

Renewing an existing license? Have your dog's current license number ready. If you do not have it, search the dog license database or contact Animal Care and Control at 617-635-5348.

Fees

The annual fee depends on whether your dog is spayed or neutered.

Annual dog license fees
Dog status Annual fee
Spayed female or neutered male (proof required) $15
Intact female or intact male $30
Owner age 70 or older No fee (mail or in person only)

Online applications include a 2.7% service fee (minimum $1.00) added at checkout.

For mail and in-person applications, pay by check or money order payable to City of Boston Animal Care and Control.

Licenses expire annually. The renewal deadline is April 1. Failure to license your dog may result in a $50 fine.

How to apply

Age 70 or older? The fee waiver is not available through the online portal — apply by mail or in person to use this waiver.

  1. 1

    Gather your documents

    Have your dog's current rabies certificate ready. If your dog is spayed or neutered, also have proof on hand — you will need it to qualify for the $15 fee.

  2. 2

    Complete the online application

    Apply at cityofboston.gov/animals/licenseapp. Payment is by credit card only. A 2.7% service fee (minimum $1.00) is added at checkout.

    The age-70+ fee waiver is not available through the online portal. Apply by mail or in person instead.

  3. 3

    Receive your license by mail

    Your license will be mailed to you after processing.

Apply online

  1. 1

    Gather your documents and clear any outstanding fines

    Collect your dog's rabies certificate and, if your dog is altered, proof of spay or neuter. Any outstanding Animal Care and Control fines must be cleared before your application can be processed.

  2. 2

    Obtain and complete the application form

    Download or request a printed application from boston.gov/departments/animal-care-and-control or by calling 617-635-5348.

  3. 3

    Mail your completed form with payment

    Include a check or money order payable to Boston Animal Care and Control. Mail to:

    1010 Massachusetts Avenue
    Boston, MA 02118

You can apply at the administrative office or the animal shelter. Hours differ between the two locations — see Get help for addresses and hours. Contact the office to confirm current visit requirements before traveling.

After you apply

For online and mail applications, your license will be mailed after your application is processed. For questions about your application status, contact Animal Care and Control:

617-635-5348  ·  animalcontrol@boston.gov
