Dog License

Boston residents must license their dogs starting at six months of age and renew the license each year before April 1. Apply online, by mail, or in person.

Boston requires residents to license their dogs each year. A dog license helps identify your pet if they get lost and supports animal control and public health efforts. You may be fined $50 if your dog is not licensed. Learn more about Boston's pet ownership rules.

Who can apply

Boston residents can apply for a new dog license or renew an existing one. Dogs must be licensed starting at six months of age.

Before you apply

Before you apply, make sure you have:

  • A current rabies certificate for your dog.
  • Proof that your dog is spayed or neutered if you want the reduced fee.
  • Your dog's license number if you're renewing.
  • Payment for the license fee and any outstanding Animal Care and Control fines.

You must license your dog each year before April 1. Search the dog license database if you're unsure whether your dog is licensed.

Residents aged 70 or older must apply by mail or in person to receive the fee waiver.

Required documents

Everyone must provide:

  • A copy of your dog's current rabies certificate.

You might need:

  • Proof that your dog is spayed or neutered to qualify for the reduced fee.
  • Your dog's existing license number if you're renewing.

Fees

Annual dog license fees
Description Amount
Spayed or neutered dog (proof required) $15
Intact dog $30
Boston residents aged 70 or older who apply by mail or in person No license fee

Online: 2.7% service fee, with a $1 minimum.

Mail and in-person payments must be made by check or money order payable to the confirmed payee.

Outstanding Animal Care and Control fines must be paid before a license can be issued.

Choose one of the following options.

    Gather your dog's current rabies certificate and license number if renewing. Prepare proof of spaying or neutering if you want the reduced fee.

  2. 2

    Complete the online application. Payment is by credit card only. A 2.7% service fee (minimum $1.00) is added at checkout.

  3. 3

    Pay the license fee and service fee by credit card.

  4. 4

    Animal Care and Control will mail your dog's license after processing. Residents aged 70 or older must apply by mail or in person to receive the fee waiver.

  3. 3

After you apply

Animal Care and Control will mail your dog's license after processing your application. For questions or a delayed license, contact 617-635-5348 or animalcontrol@boston.gov.

Renewals

Renew your dog's license each year before April 1. Have your dog's current license number and rabies certificate ready. If you don't know the number, search the dog license database. The annual fee applies, and outstanding Animal Care and Control fines must be paid.

Tag replacement

If your dog's license tag is lost or damaged, you can get a replacement tag for $8. Contact Animal Care and Control to request one.

Changes

No longer own a dog? Email animalcontrol@boston.gov or call 617-635-5348 to ask Animal Care and Control to update its records. For questions about your application or license, contact the department by phone or email.
